ejGames puts the games on your PC in one place, fetches their art, and keeps your hours and achievements. The whole library interface is a **theme**. The one that ships, Moneda, can be retuned without code, and you can build your own with HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

The logo is a coin. Three shapes come out of it, and each one always means the same thing. If you understand the logo, you understand the app.

- The **disc** is Play. There is one per screen, so you always know which button to press.
- The **ring** is progress: achievements, downloads, controller battery. Red while something is moving, white when it is still.
- The **slot** is where you are: under the open tab, and under the game selected with a controller. It is the same bar the coin has.

## Highlights

- **Finds your games** and the right `.exe` in any folder, however deep it sits.
- **Explore and Downloads**: a repack store with a built-in torrent client and an **Install** button that puts the game in your library.
- **Achievements** for standalone games, with **in-game notices** styled like each platform.
- **A controller, from start to finish**, including Big Picture.
- **Light**: while you play it can stay in the tray at about 5 MB.
