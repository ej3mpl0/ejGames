---
title: "What a theme can do"
description: "The iframe's isolation: what is allowed and what is not."
icon: "shield-halved"
---

Themes run in an **isolated iframe** (`null` origin, without `allow-same-origin`)
and with a strict security policy:

* ✅ Any HTML/CSS/JS you want, animations, canvas, WebGL, video.
* ✅ Images and videos from the library (`http://ejg-media.localhost`) and the theme's own files in their tags (`<img>`, `<link>`, `<script>`, fonts, audio, video) or with a JS module `import`.
* ❌ `fetch()` of the theme's files: `fetch` only reaches `ejg-media`. For data, use a JS module (`export default {…}`).
* ❌ The internet (fetch, external fonts or images): put what you need in the theme folder.
* ❌ Access to the disk, to other programs or to Tauri: only what `window.ejg` offers.
* ❌ `localStorage`/`IndexedDB`: use `ejg.storage`.

That is why installing someone else's theme is safe. At most it can launch games
from your library, open launcher screens, or manage downloads (only in the
downloads folder, or in one you choose in the dialog, and the installer always
asks for administrator permission).
